# 09 — APTITUDE SEQUENCE

> **Purpose:** Build enough quantitative, verbal, non-verbal and analytical aptitude ability to perform reliably in campus internship and placement assessments without allowing aptitude preparation to consume disproportionate time from Java, DSA and backend development.

---

# 1. Aptitude's Role

Aptitude is **not the primary career skill**.

It is a placement-enabling layer.

The roadmap therefore follows:

```text
Build foundation
      ↓
Learn common patterns
      ↓
Solve representative questions
      ↓
Timed practice
      ↓
Company-style mixed tests
      ↓
Revision
```

The two available books are the primary syllabus references:

1. **Fast Track OBJECTIVE ARITHMETIC — Rajesh Verma — Arihant**
2. **A New Approach to REASONING — BS Sijwali & Indu Sijwali — Arihant**

---

# 2. Aptitude Mastery Classification

| Level                   | Meaning                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 MASTER               | Fast, reliable and largely automatic under time pressure          |
| 🟠 STRONG UNDERSTANDING | Solve standard questions independently                            |
| 🟡 LEARN / USE          | Understand the method and solve representative questions          |
| 🟢 SKIM                 | Know the basic idea; low placement priority                       |
| ⚪ SKIP FOR NOW          | Do not spend preparation time unless a target company requires it |

Aptitude mastery is **speed + accuracy**, not theoretical depth.

# 13. Aptitude Introduction Timing

Aptitude should **not dominate the initial Java/DSA habit-building phase**.

The intended progression is:

```text
Early roadmap
↓
Java + DSA habit
↓
Basic aptitude introduced
↓
small regular sessions
↓
arithmetic foundation
↓
reasoning foundation
↓
timed practice
↓
company-style tests
↓
high-frequency revision
```

---

# 14. Aptitude Frequency

Early:

> approximately 1–2 sessions/week

Middle:

> approximately 2–3 sessions/week

Placement preparation:

> regular timed practice + mixed tests

Near assessment periods:

> company-specific practice where relevant

---

# 15. Aptitude Notes

Do **not** create enormous theoretical notes.

Maintain:

* formulas
* shortcuts
* recurring patterns
* traps
* mistakes
* representative questions
* speed techniques

---

# 16. Aptitude Revision

```text
Topic learned
 ↓
20–30 representative questions
 ↓
mistake logging
 ↓
weekly recall
 ↓
mixed practice
 ↓
timed test
 ↓
revisit weak topics
```

---

# 17. Final Aptitude Principle

> Aptitude preparation exists to prevent an assessment from becoming a bottleneck. It must be efficient, pattern-based and increasingly timed—not an endless second degree in mathematics.
