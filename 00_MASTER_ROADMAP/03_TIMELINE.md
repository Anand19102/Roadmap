# 17. THE WEEKLY COMPLETION RULE

The weekly target is the real unit of accountability.

If:

* Monday goes badly,
* Tuesday goes well,
* Wednesday is busy,
* Thursday is a gym day,
* Friday is tiring,

the week is **not failed**.

Saturday and Sunday can absorb unfinished work.

However:

> Catch-up is for recovering reasonable deviations, not for repeatedly compensating for chronic over-scheduling.

If the same target is repeatedly missed, the execution plan must be recalibrated.
