# 7. FIVE-TIER MASTERY SYSTEM

The permanent mastery system remains:

| Level                   | Meaning                        | What you actually do                                                |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 MASTER               | Core foundation                | Understand → reproduce → implement → solve → explain → recall later |
| 🟠 STRONG UNDERSTANDING | Important supporting knowledge | Understand → implement/use → explain main idea                      |
| 🟡 LEARN / USE          | Functional knowledge           | Know what it is, when to use it, and how to use it                  |
| 🟢 SKIM                 | Low immediate priority         | Recognize the idea and move on                                      |
| ⚪ SKIP FOR NOW          | Deliberately deferred          | Do not spend current study time on it                               |

## Critical rule

A topic does **not** become 🔴 because:

* a video was watched;
* notes were written;
* a solution was copied;
* it was understood once.

🔴 means you can **retrieve and use the knowledge independently**.
