# 16. SLIDING WINDOW — WHAT TO MASTER

Do not memorize a single code template.

Understand the concept.

## 🔴 MASTER

### Fixed window

A window has a fixed size:

```text
[left ........ right]
<---- k ---->
```

As `right` moves:

* add the new element;
* remove the element leaving the window;
* maintain the window's state.

---

### Variable window

The window expands and shrinks according to a condition:

```text
left →        ← right
```

Typical reasoning:

```text
expand right
      ↓
window becomes invalid
      ↓
move left
      ↓
restore validity
      ↓
update answer
```

---

### Most important mental model

Ask:

> **What information does the current window maintain?**

and:

> **What condition makes the window invalid?**

That is more important than memorizing code.
